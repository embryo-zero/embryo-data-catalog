# Embryo Data Catalog

Embryo Data Catalog is Embryo-Zero's fork of [OpenMetadata](https://github.com/open-metadata/OpenMetadata) (d9fb1341, commit d9fb1341). It does catalog and lineage of all data.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
